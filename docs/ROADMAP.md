Candidates after M01, depending on its cost figures: C-BIOS boot with a
TMS9918 renderer (screens 1 and 2), PSG on the OPN's SSG, keyboard and
joystick through the PPI, 16/32 KiB cartridges; then per-title patches
for VRAM loops and mapper writes.
